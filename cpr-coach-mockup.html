CPR Coach — design mockup

Three separate CPR algorithms (Adults / Children > 1 yr / Babies < 1 yr). The red circle is a metronome — it pulses at 110/min (with sound in the app) and the number inside is a timer showing how long CPR has been running. No cycle/compression counters, no pauses — continuous compressions, per the latest guidance. Rescue breaths are kept as concise optional guidance.

This version fixes Ben's note: the babies blue "Breaths" box now holds only the breaths sequence (matching Children). The baby-specific breathing technique (mouth-and-nose seal) has moved into the gray technique table, next to Hands and Depth — because it's a "how-to", like the other populations' technique.

Two fingers (index & middle) on centre of chest, between nipples; baby on a firm surface
Depth
about one third of the chest
Breaths
Seal your mouth over the baby's mouth AND nose; blow gently for 1 second and watch the chest rise. Up to 5 attempts to get 2 effective breaths.
