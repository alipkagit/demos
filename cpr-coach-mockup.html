CPR Coach — design mockup

Three separate CPR algorithms. The red circle is a metronome (pulses at 110/min, with sound in the app); the number inside is a timer for how long CPR has run. No cycle/compression counters, no pauses — continuous compressions. Rescue breaths are concise optional guidance.

Ben's fix applied: the babies blue "Breaths" box now holds only the breaths sequence (matching Children); the baby breathing technique (mouth-and-nose seal) moved into the gray technique table beside Hands & Depth.
